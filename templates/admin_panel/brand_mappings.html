How brand mappings work

The schedule uses Brand names. LMRB Theme connects to monitoring data. TC Theme(s) connect to the Transmission Certificate - multiple TC names can map to one brand row. Select Channel → Month → Schedule to filter the dropdowns to just the data you need.
