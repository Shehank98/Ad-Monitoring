Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Phoenix O & M (Pvt) Ltd ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our Ad-Monitoring platform and receive WhatsApp notifications through it.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information to operate the Ad-Monitoring service:

  • Name and email address (for staff and channel officer accounts)
  • WhatsApp phone number (for channel officers who receive automated alerts)
  • Advertising schedule and monitoring data uploaded by authorised staff
  • Login activity and session information for security purposes

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Send automated WhatsApp notifications about missed ad spots and TC upload reminders
  • Authenticate and authorise users to access the platform
  • Generate advertising reconciliation reports for campaign management
  • Maintain platform security and audit logs

3. WhatsApp Messaging

We use the Meta WhatsApp Business Cloud API to send automated notifications to registered channel officers. By providing your WhatsApp number to Phoenix O & M, you consent to receive these automated messages. You can opt out at any time by contacting us at the email address below.

We do not sell, rent, or share your WhatsApp number or any personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Phone numbers are shared with Meta solely to deliver the messages you have consented to receive.

4. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to provide the service. Staff and officer account data is removed upon request or when the account is deleted. Advertising campaign data is retained for the duration required by our clients' contracts.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data against unauthorised access, alteration, or disclosure. Access to the platform is restricted to authorised personnel only.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent to receive WhatsApp notifications at any time
